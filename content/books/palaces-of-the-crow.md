Palaces of the Crow is the second Ray Nayler book I read. It's set in Lithuania
during the Second World War, where four young teens caught between the Nazis and
the Red Army survive a winter in the woods with the help of a flock of highly
intelligent crows. I finished The Mountain in the Sea last year and really
enjoyed it. He is a good writer, and his ability to weave theories of
consciousness with a compelling story makes these books a joy to read. Palaces
of the Crow is no exception.

That said, this was a harder read than The Mountain in the Sea for me. The story
reveals itself very slowly, and during the middle parts feels like a bit of a
slog. A lot of the emotional depth felt repetitive, going over the same things
again and again instead of exploring new territory.

Overall I would recommend it, but I wish it covered more ground than it did.
