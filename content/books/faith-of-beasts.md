This is the second book in The Captive's War, following *The Mercy of Gods*.

It is James S.A. Corey. I loved every single Expanse book, and I love this series
too, so I am not exactly a neutral party here.

What they do well, they keep doing. The alien civilisations are actually alien,
which sounds like a low bar until you notice how rarely anyone clears it. Most
science fiction gives you humans with a different number of limbs. This gives you
something whose reasoning you have to work at. Alongside that there is a full
imagining of how a society and an economy would actually hold together under
these conditions, and none of it comes at the cost of the pace. It is a thrill
ride the whole way through.

Not much else to say. It is pure, holistic, good science fiction, and I will read
the next one the week it comes out.
